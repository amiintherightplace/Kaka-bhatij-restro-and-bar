Flags overhead, lights strung across, a full bar in the corner

Kaka Vatij is a casual, easygoing restro and bar at Aapgachi Shanti Chowk. Sit on the rooftop, out on the terrace, or inside by the teal-lit bar. Groups, families, solo diners and visitors passing through all end up here.

Order small plates and quick bites with a beer, or settle in for dinner with table service. Reservations are accepted, and the private dining room is there when the table gets bigger.
